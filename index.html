Clarify

Read a scientific paper with a pencil first.

How to use

Three passes

Scientists rarely read a paper top to bottom once. They read it three times, each time for something different.

  2. Select words in the paper, then pick a tool. Each highlighter color means one thing.

    • Claimwhat they say is true1
    • Evidencenumbers, data2
    • Methodwhat they did3
    • LostI don't follow4
    • Underlineworth rereadingU
    • Circlea word I can't defineO
    • Callout boxwith a margin noteB
    • Questionsticky noteQ
    • Bracketa whole paragraph[

  3. An index card waits at the end of each section. Write one or two sentences without looking. Then check.

          • It asks what you think before it explains.

            Reading with the keyboard
            
  
            
    
            Tab
            Move from paragraph to paragraph.
            
    
            Enter
            Start selecting words in the paragraph you're on.
            
    
            ← →
            Move one word.
            
    
            Shift + ← →
            Stretch the selection.
            
    
            1–4
            Highlight: claim, evidence, method, lost.
            
    
            U O B Q
            Underline, circle, callout box, question.
            
    
            [
            Bracket the whole paragraph.
            
    
            X
            Erase the marks in the paragraph you're on (asks first).
            
    
            R
            Reading ruler on or off.
            
    
            Esc
            Stop selecting.
